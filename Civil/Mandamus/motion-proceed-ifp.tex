\section*{Certificate of Interested Persons}

The undersigned certifies, pursuant to Fifth Circuit Rules 27.4 and 28.2.1, that the persons and entities interested in the outcome of this case are those identified in the Certificate of Interested Persons in the accompanying petition.

\section*{Basis for Relief}

This proceeding is a petition under Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 21. The current Fifth Circuit fee schedule requires a \$600 docket fee for an original proceeding.

Petitioner filed an application to proceed in forma pauperis in the district court on June 12, 2026. The clerk entered that application as Dkt.\ 3 on June 23. The district court has neither granted nor denied it. Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 24(a)(3) therefore does not authorize Petitioner to proceed in this Court on the basis of a prior grant of IFP status. The pending character of Dkt.\ 3 is part of the delay that the petition asks this Court to address.

The district-court financial affidavit submitted under 28 U.S.C.\ \S\ 1915(a)(1) establishes that prepayment of fees and costs would prevent Petitioner from meeting ordinary living expenses. Petitioner's financial condition has not improved since that affidavit was executed. Petitioner remains unable to prepay the \$600 fee for this original proceeding.

The district court restricted public access to the unredacted financial affidavit. Attached as Exhibit A are the motion seeking that restriction and the redacted sworn financial affidavits submitted in support of Dkt.\ 3. Those materials provide the financial information underlying Petitioner's district-court IFP application while omitting the residential address and limited nonparty identifying information restricted below. Petitioner will provide any additional affidavit or unredacted information in the form and manner the Clerk or Court directs.

The petition asks this Court to require the district court to decide the pending IFP application, complete the review required by 28 U.S.C.\ \S\ 1915(e)(2), and determine whether process may issue. Petitioner leaves the merits of those matters, and any later preliminary injunction, to the district court.

\clearpage
Petitioner believes the petition is taken in good faith. The petition seeks an order compelling the exercise of jurisdiction.

\section*{Relief Requested}

Petitioner respectfully requests that the Court grant leave to proceed in this original proceeding in forma pauperis and waive prepayment of the docket fee.

No Defendant has been served with process or appeared in the district-court action, and no opposing counsel is of record. Accordingly, copies of this filing have been served on the trial-court judges as required by Federal Rule of Appellate Procedure 21(a)(1).
