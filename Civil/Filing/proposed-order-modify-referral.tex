\textbf{[PROPOSED] ORDER ON PLAINTIFF'S EMERGENCY MOTION TO}\
\textbf{MODIFY REFERRAL AND FOR PROMPT DISPOSITION OF}\
\textbf{DKT. 3 AND DKTS. 10--12}

Before the Court is Plaintiff's Emergency Motion to Modify Referral and for Prompt Disposition of Dkt. 3 and Dkts. 10--12. The Court has considered the Motion and the record.

\textbf{IT IS ORDERED} that the Motion is GRANTED to the following extent:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Dkt. 3 and Dkts. 10--12 shall be disposed of by August 18, 2026, if practicable. The Court may modify or withdraw the existing referrals as necessary to accomplish that disposition.

\item If disposition cannot be completed by August 18, 2026, any remaining task or submission required from Plaintiff shall be identified at once, and the earliest practicable completion date shall be set.

\item The IFP application shall be decided, and review under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2) shall be completed in accordance with 28 U.S.C. § 636(b), including submission of any necessary report and recommendation to the District Court. Any resulting process and service orders shall then be entered.
\end{enumerate}

This Order does not determine the outcome of the IFP application or review under 28 U.S.C. § 1915(e)(2). All other referrals and provisions of the Court's docket-management order remain unchanged.
