\section*{Attachment 1\\Declaration in Support of Motion for Appointment of Counsel}

I, \FilingPlaintiffName, declare under penalty of perjury as follows:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item I am the Plaintiff in this action. I submit this declaration in support of my motion for appointment of counsel.

    \item I am financially unable to retain counsel. My financial circumstances are described in my Application to Proceed in Forma Pauperis and supporting financial affidavit. I cannot afford hourly counsel, litigation retainers, or non-contingency representation.

    \item I have made diligent efforts to obtain counsel. Those efforts are summarized in Attachment 2.

    \item Despite these efforts, I have not been able to secure representation. Attorneys and organizations either did not respond, declined, lacked capacity, could not represent an individual plaintiff, or required fees and retainers I could not afford.

    \item This case involves federal disability-discrimination, retaliation, due-process, and First Amendment claims against a public university and individual state actors. It also requires handling university accommodation, grievance, and Student Conduct records; confidential student information; witness evidence; disability-related facts and records; and damages tied to enrollment, services, financial aid, and academic progress.

    \item My disability-related limitations materially impair my ability to litigate this case pro se against represented state actors. I can draft pleadings and organize records, but I cannot adequately handle the procedural and evidentiary demands of this case without counsel, including discovery, identifying and interviewing witnesses, confidentiality issues, and timely responses to dispositive motions.

    \item I request appointment of counsel under 28 U.S.C. \S{} 1915(e)(1) and any other authority the Court deems applicable. If the Court requests them, I can provide additional records about my medical condition, disability, finances, or efforts to obtain counsel. Detailed medical records contain sensitive health information, and I can submit them separately under seal or as the Court directs.
\end{enumerate}

I declare under penalty of perjury that the foregoing is true and correct.

\section*{Attachment 3\\Request to Adjust Rule 12 Briefing}

Plaintiff also requests that, if any Defendant files a motion under Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12 before the Court has ruled on this Motion for Appointment of Counsel, the Court stay or adjust Plaintiff's response deadline until after this motion is resolved, or grant such other reasonable extension as the Court deems appropriate. Good cause exists because Plaintiff is proceeding pro se and in forma pauperis, has made sustained efforts to obtain counsel, and the anticipated Rule 12 issues are likely to include qualified immunity, individual-capacity liability under 42 U.S.C. \S{} 1983, ADA Title II, Section 504, and the interaction between University grievance records and federal constitutional claims. Plaintiff does not seek delay for its own sake; he seeks a fair opportunity to respond if dispositive briefing begins before the Court resolves whether counsel should be requested under 28 U.S.C. \S{} 1915(e)(1).
